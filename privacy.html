Privacy Policy · سياسة الخصوصية

Last updated: 2026-11-24 · آخر تحديث: 2026-11-24

What the app does

Shahn Saree3 Publisher uploads videos created by the channel owner to the owner's own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram professional account. Its only user is the channel owner.

Data it accesses

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos (youtube.upload) and to read the owner's own channel name and ID (youtube.readonly).
  • Facebook / Instagram (Meta): permission to list the owner's Pages, publish posts and Reels to the owner's Page and Instagram account, and read their basic metrics.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's private computer, in files readable by the owner alone. Data obtained from Google or Meta is used only to publish the owner's videos and to show the owner how they perform. It is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. The app does not collect information about viewers or any other person.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time at Google Account → Third-party access or Facebook → Business integrations. Revoking access and deleting the token files removes all stored access data.

Contact

Through the channel: YouTube · Facebook · Instagram
